Emergency & Legal SOS Guide

What to do immediately if detained, harassed, or facing human rights abuses in Nigeria.

1. Stay Calm & Polite Do not argue aggressively or resist physical officers violently. Insist on your rights respectfully.
2. Record or Witness Under Section 39, recording public officials discharging duties is legal as long as it doesn't obstruct justice.
3. Right to Counsel You have the right to remain silent until you consult a legal practitioner (Section 36(6)).

Official Legal Citation Sources & Gazette Repositories

NaijaRights AI pulls and cross-references data from the following verified statutory sources:

  • 1999 Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (As Amended): Specifically Chapter IV (Sections 33 to 46) on Fundamental Human Rights and subsequent alteration acts passed by the National Assembly and gazetted in the Federal Republic of Nigeria Official Gazette.
  • Administration of Criminal Justice Act (ACJA, 2015): Regulating arrest procedures, prohibition of warrantless arrests for civil wrongs, and mandatory notification of legal counsel.
  • Nigeria Police Act (2020): Guidelines on stop-and-search, body search rights, and prohibited police practices.
  • National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) Act: Statutory protection and investigation guidelines.
